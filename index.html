Questions before you start.

What should I send to get started?

Share the product or material, approximate quantity and delivery country. A drawing, photo, specification or required date helps if you have one.

How are sourcing fees agreed?

Fees depend on the work you need, such as a shortlist, quote comparison or order support. We agree the scope and fees before work starts and flag separate supplier or third-party costs.

Which countries can you source from?

We consider international markets based on manufacturing capability, order quantity, destination and applicable requirements. We discuss suitable markets after reviewing your brief.

Can you help with small runs or custom orders?

It depends on the material, tooling and manufacturer’s minimum order. We can ask about samples or pilot runs and compare their cost and lead time.

Who carries out quality inspections?

Where available, we coordinate independent third-party inspections against your agreed checklist. The inspection provider carries out the work and you decide whether the evidence is sufficient.

Who handles supplier payments, freight and customs?

Supplier payments follow the terms you approve with the manufacturer. Freight and customs are handled by the parties designated under the agreed trade terms. We can clarify those responsibilities during sourcing.

What do you need to source?

Start with the product, quantity and destination. You can attach drawings or specifications in WhatsApp.
